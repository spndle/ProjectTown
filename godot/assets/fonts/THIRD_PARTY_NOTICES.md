from release `2026.08.11` of the
[TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font)
project.

The font is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete

under `licenses/`.

The ProjectTown-specific filename is
`fusion-pixel-12px-proportional-zh-hans.ttf`; its upstream filename is
`fusion-pixel-12px-proportional-zh_hans.ttf`.

The original ProjectTown town, building, environment, courier, and portrait
pixel-art assets under `godot/assets/pixel_town/` were generated specifically
